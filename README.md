# Kannada Raaga (ಕನ್ನಡ ರಾಗ)

**Kannada Raaga** is a Material You (`390×844` Android-first) Kannada music streaming web & mobile application dedicated exclusively to Kannada music (1934–2026).

## 4-Pillar Kannada Music Heritage Archive (1,906 Preloaded + 200,000+ Cloud Archive)
1. **🎬 Sandalwood Film Songs (1934–2026)**: 25,000+ film songs across 5,000+ Kannada films — from Golden Era classics (*Bedara Kannappa*, *Bangarada Manushya*, *Kasturi Nivasa*, *Naagarahaavu*, *Gandhada Gudi*, *Premaloka*, *Amruthavarshini*, *Om*) to modern blockbusters (*Mungaru Male*, *Raajakumara*, *KGF 1 & 2*, *Kantara*, *Sapta Sagaradaache Ello*, *Krishnam Pranaya Sakhi*, *Kaatera*, *Su From So*).
2. **🪔 Devotional, Dasa Sahitya, Vachanas & Carnatic**: *Purandara Dasa* & *Kanaka Dasa* Keerthanegalu, *Basavanna* & *Akka Mahadevi* Vachanas, Carnatic kritis, and Karnataka temple devotional recordings.
3. **🥁 Folk & Traditional (`Janapada Geethegalu` & `Bhavageethe`)**: Karnataka oral folk traditions, *Kolata*, *Kamsale*, *Shishunala Sharif* Tatvapada, and Rashtrakavi *Kuvempu*, *D. R. Bendre*, *K. S. Narasimhaswamy*, and *C. Ashwath* Bhavageethe.
4. **🎸 Modern Independent, Pop, Rap & Rock**: *The Raghu Dixit Project*, *Vasu Dixit*, *Swarathma*, Kannada Rap/Hip-Hop (*Chandan Shetty*, *All OK*, *Rahul Dit-O*), and independent Kannada pop singles.

## Running Locally
```bash
python serve_raaga.py
```
Then open:
- `http://localhost:8090/mobile/web_preview/index.html`
